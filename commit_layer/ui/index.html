CIEAV is the commit layer for the internet: an always-on local control plane between digital intent and consequence.

It sits between a human, AI agent, or automation and browser actions that can create real-world effects. Deterministic safety and final authority stay local.

02 Commit:Commit is not success. Observe the outcome... RECEIPT V3

A browser submission is not proof that the intended consequential action succeeded.

FAILUREOBSERVED_FAILURE — outcome verified locally

Redirects and navigation alone do not prove consequential success. The local receipt API independently gates UNDONE.

06 Security:Deterministic safety floor, conservative failure... 12 INVARIANTS

CIEAV fails conservative. Semantic uncertainty never silently becomes a safer policy.

01Website content is hostile.
02Raw secret and form values never enter cloud/model context.
03AI cannot lower the deterministic safety floor.
04HIGH, CRITICAL, and sensitive actions never habituate into passive mode.
05Approved replay is coordinate-bound, single-use, and short-lived.
06COMMITTED is not a remote-success claim.
07UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN; navigation is not proof.
08Undo requires observed success plus one credible live inverse.
