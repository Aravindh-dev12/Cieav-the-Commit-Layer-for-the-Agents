Why agents fail in the real world

Four problems, and what Cieav does about each.

Irreversible

A hallucinated rm -rf or a sent payment cannot be taken back.

Cieav gives every tool an exact inverse. /undo restores byte for byte.

Cloud prison

Remote sandboxes lack your cookies, 2FA sessions, desktop apps and private files.

Cieav runs on your host and attaches to your real browser session.

Slow and costly

Re-reasoning every click through a cloud model takes 2 to 5 seconds and burns tokens.

Cieav replays cached playbooks in about 50ms with no model call.

Unaccountable

Plain text logs can be edited, so nobody can prove what ran.

Cieav signs every action with Ed25519 and chains receipts with SHA-256.
